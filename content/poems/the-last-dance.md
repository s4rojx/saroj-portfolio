---
title: "The Last Dance"
date: "2026-11-11"
tags: ["distance", "silence", "closure"]
description: "A poem about two souls drifting apart without words, stepping back into the shadows as the final melody fades."
---

Her window lamp went dim one night,
And he did not climb the stair.
He told himself a house needs rest,
And left her darkness there.

He lowered his own light as well,
So she would never see him wait.
Two houses on one sleeping street,
Each pretending it was late.

The tide that used to reach his feet
Now turns back far from shore.
He walks the wet and empty sand
And counts the shells once more.

A faint old song is playing on,
The one they used to know.
Two shadows on the empty floor
Keep their distance, soft and slow.

If this is the final turn,
He will not hold the room.
He will bow and let the lights go down
And leave the song to the gloom.
